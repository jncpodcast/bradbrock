About Brad

A working musician.
A one-of-a-kind show.

Brad Brock is a South Florida singer-songwriter and one man band entertainer whose live show turns a compact setup into a full-band experience.

His mix of pop, rock, blues, reggae and original music is arranged for real-time performance — no loop pedals, no pre-recorded backing tracks, and no waiting around between songs. The result is polished enough for resorts and corporate events, lively enough for festivals and bars, and unmistakably live.

Over more than two decades of performing, Brad has played venues and events across Florida and brought his show aboard cruise ships around the world.
